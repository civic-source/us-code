# § 79d. Limitation on administrative and supervisory costs

The total administrative and supervisory costs which may be incurred under this chapter for services performed (excluding standardization, compliance, and foreign monitoring activities) for each of the fiscal years 1989 through 2025 1 1 See Application of Section During Certain Years note below. shall not exceed 30 percent of the total costs for such activities carried out by the Secretary for such year.
