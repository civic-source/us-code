# § 6881. Definitions

In this chapter:

  - **(1)** The term “exclusive economic zone” has the meaning given that term in section 107 of title 46 .
  - **(2)** The term “fishing restriction” means a restriction on fishing in a federally managed fishery established under section 1853(b)(2) of this title , including—
    - **(A)** a full or partial fishing closure, in terms of both area and duration, or a seasonal closure;
    - **(B)** a no-catch zone in the fishery;
    - **(C)** a restriction on the method of catch for the fishery; and
    - **(D)** another restriction on fishing, as determined by the Secretary.
  - **(3)** The terms “Indian Tribe” and “Tribal organization” have the meanings given those terms in section 5304 of title 25 .
  - **(4)** The term “Native Hawaiian organization” has the meaning given the term in section 7517 of title 20 , except that the term includes the Department of Hawaiian Home Lands and the Office of Hawaiian Affairs of the State of Hawaii.
  - **(5)** The term “nonprofit organization” means an organization that is described in section 501(c) of title 26 and exempt from taxation under section 501(a) of such title.
  - **(6)** The term “recreational vessel” has the meaning given that term in section 2101 of title 46 .
  - **(7)** The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Commerce.
