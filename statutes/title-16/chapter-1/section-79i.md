# § 79i. Administration

The Secretary shall administer the Redwood National Park in accordance with the provisions of the Act of August 25, 1916 ( 39 Stat. 535 , 16 U.S.C. 1–4 ), 1 1 See References in Text note below. as amended and supplemented.
