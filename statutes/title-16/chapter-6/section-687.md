# § 687. Grand Canyon Game Preserve included in park

Such parts of the Grand Canyon National Game Preserve, designated under authority of section 684 of this title , as are by this Act included with 1 1 So in original. Probably should be “within”. the Grand Canyon National Park are excluded and eliminated from said game preserve.
