# § 14001. Applicability of this part

This chapter and chapters 1403 through 1411 of this title apply, as appropriate, to all reserve officers of the Army, Navy, Air Force, and Marine Corps except warrant officers.


Promotions To Fill Vacancies

Promotions Other Than To Fill Vacancies

Officers Found Qualified for Promotion to First Lieutenant

Officers Once Failed of Selection

Officers Twice Failed of Selection

Officers With Approved Promotion Declinations in Effect

Covered Officers

Recommendations for Promotion

Failures of Selection

Officers Other Than Covered Officers Recommended for Promotion

Officers Found Qualified for Promotion to Lieutenant (Junior Grade) or First Lieutenant

Officers Whose Names Have Been Omitted From a List Furnished to a Selection Board

Covered Officers
