# § 9151. Commissioned officer grades

The commissioned grades in the Regular Air Force and in the Space Force are:

  - **(1)** Major general.
  - **(2)** Brigadier general.
  - **(3)** Colonel.
  - **(4)** Lieutenant colonel.
  - **(5)** Major.
  - **(6)** Captain.
  - **(7)** First lieutenant.
  - **(8)** Second lieutenant.
