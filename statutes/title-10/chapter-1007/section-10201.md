# § 10201. Assistant Secretary of Defense for Manpower and Reserve Affairs

As provided in section 138(b)(2) 1 1 See References in Text note below. of this title, the official in the Department of Defense with responsibility for overall supervision of reserve affairs of the Department of Defense is the Assistant Secretary of Defense for Manpower and Reserve Affairs.
