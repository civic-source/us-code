# § 1527. Fish and wildlife conservation and development

The conservation and development of the fish and wildlife resources and the enhancement of recreation opportunities in connection with the project works authorized pursuant to this subchapter shall be in accordance with the provisions of the Federal Water Project Recreation Act ( 79 Stat. 213 ) [ 16 U.S.C. 460 l –12 et seq.], except as provided in section 1522 of this title .
