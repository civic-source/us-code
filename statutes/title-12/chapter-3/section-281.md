# § 281. Capital

No Federal reserve bank shall commence business with a subscribed capital less than $4,000,000.

*(Dec. 23, 1913, ch. 6, § 2 (part), [38 Stat. 253](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/statute/38/253).)*
