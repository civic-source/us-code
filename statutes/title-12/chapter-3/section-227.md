# § 227. “Banking Act of 1933”

The short title of the Act of June 16, 1933, ch. 89 , 48 Stat. 162 , shall be the “Banking Act of 1933.”


## Source Credit

*(June 16, 1933, ch. 89, § 1, [48 Stat. 162](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/statute/48/162).)*
