# § 226. “Federal Reserve Act”

The short title of the Act of December 23, 1913, ch. 6 , 38 Stat. 251 , shall be the “Federal Reserve Act.”

*(Dec. 23, 1913, ch. 6, § 1 (par.), [38 Stat. 251](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/statute/38/251).)*
