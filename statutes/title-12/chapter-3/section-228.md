# § 228. “Banking Act of 1935”

The act of August 23, 1935, ch. 614 , 49 Stat. 684 , may be cited as the “Banking Act of 1935.”


## Source Credit

*(Aug. 23, 1935, ch. 614, § 1, [49 Stat. 684](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/statute/49/684).)*
