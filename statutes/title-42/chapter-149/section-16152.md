# § 16152. Definitions

In this subchapter:

  - **(1)** The terms “clean hydrogen” and “hydrogen” mean hydrogen produced in compliance with the greenhouse gas emissions standard established under section 16166(a) of this title , including production from any fuel source.
  - **(2)** The term “fuel cell” means a device that directly converts the chemical energy of a fuel, which is supplied from an external source, and an oxidant into electricity by electrochemical processes occurring at separate electrodes in the device.
  - **(3)** The term “heavy-duty vehicle” means a motor vehicle that—
    - **(A)** is rated at more than 8,500 pounds gross vehicle weight;
    - **(B)** has a curb weight of more than 6,000 pounds; or
    - **(C)** has a basic vehicle frontal area in excess of 45 square feet.
  - **(4)** The term “infrastructure” means the equipment, systems, or facilities used to produce, distribute, deliver, or store hydrogen (except for onboard storage).
  - **(5)** The term “light-duty vehicle” means a motor vehicle that is rated at 8,500 or less pounds gross vehicle weight.
  - **(6)** The terms “portable” and “storage”, when used in reference to a fuel cell, include—
    - **(A)** continuous electric power; and
    - **(B)** backup electric power.
  - **(7)** The term “Task Force” means the Hydrogen and Fuel Cell Technical Task Force established under section 16155 of this title .
  - **(8)** The term “Technical Advisory Committee” means the independent Technical Advisory Committee established under section 16156 of this title .
