# § 3535. Approval of courses

  - **(1)** is approved in accordance with the provisions of subchapter I 1 1 See References in Text note below. of chapter 36 of this title, or
  - **(2)** is approved for the enrollment of the particular individual under the provisions of section 3536 of this title .
