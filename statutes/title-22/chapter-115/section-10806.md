# § 10806. Prohibition on listing or trading of Russian entities on United States securities exchanges

- **(a)** Not later than 30 days after September 18, 2026 , the Securities and Exchange Commission shall prohibit the securities of an issuer described in subsection (b) from being traded on a national securities exchange.
- **(b)** An issuer described in this subsection is an issuer that is—
  - **(1)** an official of or individual affiliated with the Government of the Russian Federation; or
  - **(2)** an entity—
    - **(A)** in which the Government of the Russian Federation has a controlling or majority ownership interest; or
    - **(B)** that is otherwise affiliated with the Government of the Russian Federation.
- **(c)** In this section:
  - **(1)** The terms “issuer” and “security” have the meanings given those terms in section 78c(a) of title 15 .
  - **(2)** The term “national securities exchange” means an exchange registered as a national securities exchange in accordance with section 78f of title 15 .
