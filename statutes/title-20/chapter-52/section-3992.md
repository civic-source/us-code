# § 3992. Programs authorized

- **(a)** The Secretary may make grants to States to pay the Federal share of the cost of the programs described in section 3994 of this title .
- **(b)** There are authorized to be appropriated for purposes of carrying out this part 1 1 See References in Text note below. $20,000,000 for fiscal year 1988.
