# § 81q. Transfer of grant

The grant shall not be sold, conveyed, transferred, set over, or assigned.


## Source Credit

*(June 18, 1934, ch. 590, § 17, [48 Stat. 1002](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/statute/48/1002).)*
