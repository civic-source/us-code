# § 12107. Wrecked vessels

- **(a)** A vessel is a wrecked vessel under this chapter if it—
  - **(1)** was wrecked on a coast of the United States or adjacent waters; and
  - **(2)** has undergone repairs in a shipyard in the United States equal to at least 3 times the appraised salvage value of the vessel.
- **(b)** The Secretary may appoint a board of three appraisers to determine whether a vessel satisfies subsection (a)(2). The costs of the appraisal shall be paid by the owner of the vessel.
