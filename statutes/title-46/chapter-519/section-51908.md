# § 51908. Prohibition against unauthorized manufacture, sale, possession, or display of awards

- **(a)** Except as authorized by this chapter or the Secretary of Transportation, a person may not manufacture, sell, possess, or display a decoration or medal provided for in this chapter.
- **(b)** A person violating this section is liable to the United States Government for a civil penalty of not more than $2,000.
