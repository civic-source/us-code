# § 4403. Record requirements

Each person liable for tax under this subchapter shall keep a daily record showing the gross amount of all wagers on which he is so liable, in addition to all other records required pursuant to section 6001(a). 1 1 So in original. Section 6001 of this title does not contain subsections.
