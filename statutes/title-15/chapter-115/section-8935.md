# § 8935. Collaboration

In carrying out this subchapter, the Director shall collaborate with—

  - **(1)** appropriate Federal and State regulators;
  - **(2)** institutions of higher education;
  - **(3)** research institutions; and
  - **(4)** other expert stakeholders.
